Security🚩 Applies to You

> Prompt injection grew legs.
> It now plans, executes, iterates.

Google Cloud's security team warned this week that attackers have moved past single-prompt tricks into automated agentic chains that plan a goal, run it, check the result and try again. Their guidance: treat any agentic workflow as its own threat surface, not as a chat window with extra steps. If you are wiring Claude into your inbox, calendar and repos, this is the memo.

Physics

Einstein's gravity, caught behaving quantum-mechanically.

An international team — Ben-Gurion, Ulm and Oxford, with Roger Penrose among the authors — split an ultracold atom's wave so one half hung still while the other fell, then recombined them to measure the difference. Published September 2 in Science Advances, it is the first direct observation of the equivalence principle acting on a falling quantum object.

Genomics

DeepMind mapped nine billion DNA variants.

Google DeepMind published a map of roughly nine billion genetic variants, an effort to predict which mutations across the human genome are consequential and which are noise. Read alongside this week's separate finding that 1,200-plus variants track with the Big Five personality traits, the "genomics is a prediction problem" thesis is having a very good month.

Creator Economy🚩 Applies to You

The YPP door gets twice as heavy on February 1.

YouTube's August announcement doubles the bar for new Partner Program applicants: 1,000 subscribers plus either 8,000 public watch hours or 20 million Shorts views, with a separate 10-million-qualified-Shorts-views threshold for Shorts ad and subscription revenue. Existing partners are grandfathered but must accept updated terms by January 31, 2027 — worth putting on a slide before that deadline.

Creative Tools🚩 Applies to You

Illustrator finally got a multiplayer cursor.

Adobe's September Illustrator release adds real-time co-editing through Creative Cloud — live cursors, named presence, two or more people in one file at once. It is the feature Figma made table stakes, and its arrival in Illustrator is Adobe conceding the point rather than arguing it. Figma answered the same week with canvas-level vector erasing and scalable opacity in Variables.

Privacy

Law firms became the soft target of 2026.

Quinn Emanuel and McDermott Will & Schulte are the latest US firms to disclose breaches, adding to dozens of law-firm incidents logged this year — attackers going after the counsel rather than the client. The timing bites: California's SB 446 amendment now demands 30-day individual notice and 15-day attorney general reporting, and twenty states have comprehensive privacy statutes live as of January 1.
