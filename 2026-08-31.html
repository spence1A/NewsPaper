Sony and Warner sue Anthropic over “brazen” IP theft

Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell filed jointly in the Northern District of California on Friday, naming Anthropic along with Dario Amodei and co-founder Benjamin Mann personally. The complaint alleges a campaign of torrenting, scraping and downloading tens of thousands of copyrighted compositions to train Claude, and seeks up to $150,000 per willfully infringed work. All three major music publishers are now litigating against the same company.

Claude now ships with a browser of its own

Anthropic embedded a native browser directly inside the Claude Cowork desktop app. When a task touches the web, a browser pane opens in the side panel and Claude navigates, reads, clicks and fills forms itself — no Chrome extension, and no access to your personal browsing session. Live on Enterprise now, rolling out to Pro, Max and Team across macOS, Windows and Linux this week.

🚩 Applies to You

YouTube doubles the bar for joining the Partner Program

Starting February 1, 2027, new creators will need 8,000 qualified watch hours over 365 days or 20 million qualified Shorts views over 90 days to monetize — roughly double today's thresholds. Existing partners are unaffected, and YouTube says sub-threshold channels get Shopping bonuses, brand-deal incentives and trend-growth payouts instead. It is the sharpest narrowing of the on-ramp since YPP launched.

The view count under your video means something different now

YouTube flipped to counting a view the moment playback starts, aligning the public number with how Shorts have always been tallied. Ad revenue and YPP eligibility still run on qualified watch time and engaged views, so payouts do not change — but the headline number on every video, and every screenshot in every pitch deck, just inflated. Expect noisy year-over-year comparisons.

Science · August 28

Eye drops gave blind mice their sight back

An IBEC-led consortium published a class of photoswitchable small molecules, called prosthe6, in the Journal of the American Chemical Society. The compounds stand in for degenerated photoreceptors, and two of them worked as ordinary eye drops — completely blind mice recovered light-driven behavior within hours, under normal room lighting, with no gene therapy, implants or distress. The human targets are macular degeneration and retinitis pigmentosa.
