YouTube Doubles the Bar to Get Paid

YouTube announced on August 10 that new creators will need 8,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days — or 20 million Shorts views in 90 days — to enter the Partner Program, roughly double the current thresholds. Existing monetized channels are unaffected. The change takes effect February 1, 2027.

Claude Now Watermarks Everything It Writes

Anthropic began embedding invisible watermarks into text from new Claude models on August 2, designed to survive copy-and-paste by subtly biasing word choice. Signed C2PA provenance metadata is also being attached to generated .png, .jpg and .svg files. The signal degrades under heavy editing, paraphrasing or translation.

As of August 2026 the EU AI Act is fully applicable, carrying fines of up to 7% of global turnover for prohibited practices.
