01
Creator Economy🚩 Applies to You

YouTube doubles the bar to get paid.

The Partner Program now demands 8,000 qualified watch hours over 365 days, or 20 million Shorts views over 90 days — double the old thresholds, and the first rewrite of the revenue-share rules since 2018. Shorts creators must also hold 10 million views per 90 days to stay in the Shorts pool. New applicants face the higher bar starting February 1, 2027.

AI Platforms🚩 Applies to You

Claude ships computer use, Skills and Files to GA.

Anthropic moved computer use, the Skills API and the Files API to general availability on the Claude Platform, adding browser control for web apps and sturdier agent workflows. Claude Code landed alongside it with better cost estimates, cloud sessions and remote control. Translation: the workflows you have been duct-taping together are now supported surface area.

03
Video & Infrastructure🚩 Applies to You

Alibaba raises roughly $10B and pairs it with a new AI video model.

A share placement of about $10 billion is earmarked for computing infrastructure and the Qwen model line, announced the same week as a new AI video model. Another deep-pocketed entrant in generative video means more tools for creators — and more synthetic footage competing for the same feed.

04
Open Weights

Meta open-sources Glimmer, and it runs on one GPU.

Muse Glimmer is a 30-billion-parameter model released under Apache 2.0, small enough to run on a 24GB card via Ollama or llama.cpp with no cloud round-trip. Mark Zuckerberg framed the release as an argument that AI should not sit behind a handful of labs. The practical effect is a capable local model with no per-token meter running.

05
Security

OpenAI splits Daybreak in two and ships a cyber model.

The cyber defense service now has Blue and Red tiers, anchored by GPT-5.6-Cyber, which OpenAI says completes 95% of its cyber benchmark tasks and surfaced two Chrome V8 zero-days. It follows Anthropic’s cyber-focused Mythos release. The arms race now has offense and defense shipping from the same vendors.

06
Compute

Anthropic anchors a purpose-built US data center venture.

Theseus Infrastructure launches with Macquarie Asset Management and GIC funding the equity and Anthropic as anchor tenant. Notably, Anthropic says it will pay 100% of grid costs and cover consumer electricity increases — an attempt to answer the “AI is raising my power bill” critique before it fully lands.

07
Creative Tools🚩 Applies to You

Figma rewires auto layout spacing.

Auto layout gains two new auto-spacing modes — “Around” and “Evenly” — with the old default renamed “Between.” Admins can now also control which skills, templates, libraries and Make kits get recommended across an organization. Small change, large blast radius for anyone maintaining shared design files.

08
Privacy Law

The EU’s top court is asked how much of your phone a regulator may copy.

A Lithuanian referral put the question to the Court of Justice on August 24: during an investigation, how far can authorities go in copying data off a personal electronic device? A separate referral the same day asks whether Bulgaria’s national health information system breached EU privacy rules in transferring medical records.

09
Breach

A law firm loses the data of 36,000 clients.

A proposed class action filed in Georgia federal court alleges Troutman Pepper Locke LLP failed to protect personal data belonging to more than 36,000 clients and third parties. Separately, Utah joined a federal suit against Hims & Hers over unauthorized subscription charges and private health data shared with advertisers.

Source ↗
10
Science

Animals were eating bioplastic long before we invented it.

Researchers found that dozens of animal species carry enzymes capable of breaking down PHAs — microbial bioplastics that predate human chemistry by hundreds of millions of years. It reframes biodegradable plastic as something evolution already solved, and hands materials scientists a very old toolkit.
