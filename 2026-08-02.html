Astronomy

A rocky planet in the habitable zone kept its atmosphere.

Astronomers confirmed the first atmosphere ever detected around a rocky planet orbiting inside another star's habitable zone. LHS 1140 b, 48 light-years out, gave itself away through helium slowly leaking into space — evidence it has held an envelope for billions of years. The result published in Science and immediately became the most compelling target in the search for life.

AI Race🚩 Applies to You

OpenAI's GPT-5.6 clears a government review before shipping.

OpenAI launched GPT-5.6 in three variants — Sol, Terra and Luna — making it the first frontier model to pass a customer-by-customer US government review ahead of public release. Sol reportedly hit 750 tokens per second on Cerebras hardware. The review-then-release sequence is the part worth watching: it may become the template every lab is pushed into.

Privacy

42 state attorneys general settle the 23andMe breach.

Connecticut AG William Tong announced that a coalition of 42 state attorneys general reached a settlement with the bankruptcy trustee for 23andMe, resolving claims from the company's 2023 genetic data breach. The case is the clearest test yet of what happens to consumer data claims when the company holding the data goes under.

Health Research

One avocado a day moved LDL particle counts in a six-month trial.

A randomized trial reported August 1 found that adults with abdominal obesity who ate one avocado daily for six months showed a measurable drop in circulating LDL particle concentration — a change the authors associate with roughly a 4% reduction in estimated cardiovascular risk. Particle count, not total cholesterol, was the endpoint that moved.

Enforcement

California opens its first formal CCPA audit — on gig platforms.

The California Privacy Protection Agency launched its first formal CCPA compliance audit, targeting gig economy technology platforms operating in the state. It is the agency's first use of its audit authority rather than its enforcement-by-complaint track, and it arrives alongside amended privacy laws taking effect in Connecticut, Utah and Arkansas.

Neuroscience

A sleeping brain may flag dementia years before memory fails.

Researchers reported July 31 that machine learning applied to overnight EEG recordings can surface warning signs of dementia long before memory symptoms appear. A companion finding the same week identified a gene in the brain's overnight clearance system that appears to modulate how strongly poor sleep drives Alzheimer's-related changes.
