The aging brain calls in reinforcements from the blood.

Stanford researchers used somatic-mutation lineage tracing on matched human blood and postmortem brain tissue to show that blood immune cells cross the blood-brain barrier starting in middle age and become functional microglia. The effect is absent in mice and non-human primates — it appears to be uniquely human. Published in Nature.
