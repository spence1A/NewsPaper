Climate

A Manhattan-sized ice island tore free from Petermann Glacier

A 76.4-square-kilometre slab — possibly 150 metres thick — separated from the floating tongue of Greenland's Petermann Glacier, the glacier's largest ice loss since 2012 and the biggest Arctic calving event since 2020. ESA's Sentinel-1 caught cracks spreading along the centerline on August 3; by 20:00 UTC the next day the island was fully detached. Researchers expect two more sections to go, which would strip roughly 22% of the remaining tongue.

Medicine

A nanoparticle that lights up brain cancer, then burns what's left

Researchers at UTS, Harvard and Henan built a light-activated nanozyme that makes glioma cell clusters visible in near-infrared during surgery, then destroys the microscopic residue afterward. Platinum atoms pull oxygen from tumor hydrogen peroxide to amplify heat- and radical-mediated killing. In mice it prevented recurrence entirely, with 100% survival at 60 days — it has not been tested in humans.
