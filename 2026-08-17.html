Deals · August 16

Stripe buys OpenRouter for more than $7 billion.

Stripe finalized its acquisition of the AI model-routing gateway that sits between roughly 8 million developers and 400+ models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and DeepSeek. The price is a 5.4x markup on the $1.3 billion valuation OpenRouter carried at its Series B just three months ago. Model routing is now, officially, a payments-infrastructure problem.

Creator Economy · August 10

YouTube doubles the bar to get monetized.

Starting February 1, 2027, new creators will need 8,000 qualified watch hours over 365 days or 20 million Shorts views over 90 days to enter the Partner Program — double today's thresholds. The 1,000-subscriber floor stays. Shorts revenue now also requires holding 10 million views per rolling 90 days. It's the first structural change to revenue sharing since 2018.

AI Safety · August 14

Anthropic raises its own misalignment risk rating.

In its second company-wide Risk Report, Anthropic lifted the risk of catastrophic harm from misalignment in high-stakes settings from "very low" to "low," citing uncertainty around recent cybersecurity evaluation disclosures. The same report disclosed an unreleased internal model — "Model 2" — that outperforms its frontier model, with no current plans to ship it.

Infrastructure · August 7

Cloudflare shipped a browser with no interface.

Kitesurf is a cloud-hosted browser built for AI agents rather than people — no tabs, no themes, no extensions, because an agent doesn't need any of it. It's a bet that a meaningful share of web traffic is about to stop having a human on the other end of it.

Science · August 7

An AI wrote a working virus genome from scratch.

Stanford's Evo 2 model generated novel bacteriophage genomes starting from ΦX174. Researchers synthesized and tested nearly 300 of them; 16 proved exceptionally effective at killing antibiotic-resistant E. coli. It's the first peer-reviewed demonstration of generative AI designing entire functional viral genomes — and it has biosecurity researchers asking hard questions.

Medicine · August 16

An Alzheimer's compound stopped the clumps forming.

An experimental compound prevented damaging amyloid protein clusters from forming in the brains of mice, reducing buildup and helping nerve cells survive measurably longer. Early-stage animal work, a long way from a clinic — but it targets formation rather than clearance, which is a different bet than most of the field has been making.

Regulation · August

The EU AI Act is now fully enforceable.

The Act reached full applicability this month, and with it real teeth: fines up to 7% of global turnover for prohibited AI practices. It arrives alongside a broader compliance squeeze — 20 US states now have comprehensive privacy laws, and California's Delete Act switched on its centralized data-broker deletion system on August 1.

Security · August

CL0P's breach reaches Oracle and Estée Lauder.

Oracle and Estée Lauder were hit with litigation this month over a data breach traced to the Russian cybercriminal syndicate CL0P. It's part of a wider pattern — a separate ransomware attack on law firm Fox Rothschild drew a CCPA claim in a class action the same week. Vendor breaches are increasingly landing as customer lawsuits.
