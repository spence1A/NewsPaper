Security · Aug 11🚩 Applies to You

One Zoom participant could silently own every device on the call.

Zoom patched "Zoomsday" (CVE-2026-53413), a zero-click remote code execution flaw in the annotation feature that hit any call involving screen sharing — host or guest, on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS and Android. Researchers found and weaponized it using public AI models in under 24 hours and fewer than 20 prompts. Update to Workplace 7.1.5 or 7.0.6.

AI Security · Aug 12🚩 Applies to You

Researchers cracked the encrypted reasoning inside every major AI API.

A disclosed flaw in how OpenAI, Anthropic and Google pass hidden reasoning between API calls let researchers replay an encrypted block into a weaker sibling model, which printed the contents verbatim — including API keys and passwords recovered from session logs. All three have since changed their APIs, though some traces remain recoverable.

Supply Chain · Aug 11

A poisoned LiteLLM package reached 434,000 build pipelines.

CloudSEK reported that Team PCP compromised LiteLLM's PyPI packages 1.82.7 and 1.82.8 by way of the Trivy scanner inside its build pipeline, staying undetected for roughly 20 days. Potentially exposed: AWS, GCP and Azure credentials, SSH keys, Kubernetes tokens and LLM API keys across 2,500+ organizations including Nvidia, AWS, Cisco and Salesforce.

Physics · Aug 7

Sunlight produced quantum entanglement for the first time.

A University of Ottawa and Max Planck Institute team concentrated sunlight gathered over 1.4 square meters into a nonlinear crystal, generating polarization-entangled photons at nearly 94% fidelity with correlations that violated Bell's inequality. Published in Optica, it overturns the assumption that entanglement requires laser light.

Aviation · Aug 12

Space weather is a bigger problem for aircraft than assumed.

Surrey Space Centre researchers found that extreme solar events meaningfully raise radiation exposure at cruising altitudes of 8–14 km while triggering single-event upsets in onboard electronics — small faults that accumulate into disrupted systems. Published in the Journal of Space Weather and Space Climate.
