Astronomy · Aug 22

The Universe Was Hiding Most of Its Stars

Penn State astronomers combined JWST with Very Large Telescope data to weigh nine mature, long-dead galaxies and found a vast hidden population of small, faint stars nobody could resolve before. Those stars make the galaxies three to four times more massive than previous estimates — one formed less than 1.5 billion years after the Big Bang. Published this weekend in Nature Astronomy, it makes early galaxy formation harder to explain, not easier.

Claude Designed Real Proteins. The Lab Confirmed It.

Anthropic published wet-lab-validated results: Claude models autonomously designed protein binders that worked against 14 of 15 targets, yielding 354 confirmed binders from 1,320 designs. Hit rates ran 22–35% against an industry norm of 10–15%, and on one target Claude hit 40% where human competition entrants managed 3.7%. Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience did the independent bench work.

For the first time since 2018, YouTube rewrote the Partner Program bar: new creators will need 8,000 qualified watch hours over 365 days, or 20 million Shorts views over 90 days — roughly double the current thresholds. Most changes take effect February 1, 2027. Channels below the line get pushed toward Shopping bonuses, brand-deal incentives and trend-growth payouts instead of ad share.
