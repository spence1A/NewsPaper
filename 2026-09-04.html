Model Launch🚩 Applies to You

Anthropic ships Claude Fable 5.1 — cheaper cache, fewer refusals

Anthropic released Fable 5.1 and the gated Mythos 5.1 on September 1, calling them its most advanced models for coding and knowledge work. Infrastructure🚩 Applies to You

ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini all went dark on the same morning

Anthropic confirmed an outage beginning around 9:41 AM ET on September 3 that hit Mythos 5.1, Fable 5.1 and Opus 5. OpenAI and Google reported overlapping disruptions the same day, with Gemini taking about two hours to fully recover. A simultaneous triple failure across the three largest labs is a pointed reminder that "the AI is down" is now a single point of failure in a lot of workflows.

On September 2, OpenAI said Astra exceeded the cybersecurity threshold in its own Preparedness Framework — the first LLM to hit the Critical tier. Health Tech

ChatGPT Health plugs into Epic's medical records

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Health on September 2 with a read-only integration into Epic's electronic health record system, reaching a footprint of more than 325 million patients. Read-only is doing a lot of work in that sentence — but it's the largest consumer-AI hook into clinical records to date, and the privacy questions start now.

A Hall effect that shouldn't exist

Physicists at Carnegie Mellon reported an unexpected form of the Hall effect, overturning the long-held assumption that the electrical response only appears when a magnetic field sits perpendicular to the material.
