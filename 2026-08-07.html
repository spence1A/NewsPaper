Policy · Aug 2

The EU AI Act's disclosure rules just went live.

Article 50 became enforceable this week: chatbot disclosure, machine-readable marking of AI-generated content, and deepfake labeling are now binding, along with GPAI enforcement powers and the penalty regime. The high-risk system obligations were separately deferred to late 2027 and 2028 — but the content-labeling piece was not postponed.

Security · Aug 3–4

Hackers took the names behind 31,000 Liechtenstein entities.

Attackers breached Liechtenstein's beneficial-ownership register on the night of July 29–30, exposing names and residencies for roughly 31,000 trusts, funds and foundations. Prime Minister Brigitte Haas said a crisis unit is working "around the clock"; officials found no evidence records were altered or deleted. The register exists specifically to satisfy EU anti-money-laundering directives.

Paleontology · Aug 4

A fossil bed in the Northwest Territories moves animal life back 10 million years.

Researchers from the American Museum of Natural History and Dartmouth catalogued over 100 Ediacaran fossils — six groups never before recorded in North America — dated to 567 million years ago. The find pushes the earliest evidence of animal movement and sexual reproduction 5 to 10 million years earlier than previously established. Published in Science Advances.

Medicine · Aug 3

A bean-based chewing gum cut oral HPV by 93% in patient samples.

A Penn dental medicine team led by Henry Daniell engineered a gum from lablab beans containing FRIL, an antiviral protein. Extracts reduced HPV by 93% in saliva samples from head-and-neck cancer patients and nearly eliminated two cancer-linked bacteria while sparing beneficial oral flora. Important caveat: this is preclinical — no patient chewed the gum as treatment.
