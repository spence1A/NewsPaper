A Manhattan-sized ice island breaks off Greenland

A 76-square-kilometre slab snapped free from Greenland's Petermann Glacier, forming an ice island roughly the size of Manhattan. Satellites had tracked the cracks spreading for weeks before the calving completed. Researchers published the sequence this week as a case study in how quickly fracture propagation can run.
