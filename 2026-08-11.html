AI Policy · August 11

Anthropic will watermark every word Claude writes

Anthropic announced it will embed machine-detectable watermarks in text generated by its models, including Claude, to satisfy the EU AI Act's Transparency Code that took effect August 2. Every model released after that date carries the marking tech automatically, covering both generated text and files. If you publish Claude-assisted scripts, copy, or show notes, assume detectability is now the default.

YouTube just doubled the price of admission

New creators will now need 8,000 qualified watch hours over 365 days, or 20 million qualified Shorts views over 90 days, to enter the Partner Program — roughly double the old 4,000-hour / 10-million-view bar. It's the first structural change to revenue sharing since 2018. From February 1, 2027, channels must hold 10 million Shorts views per 90 days to keep short-form monetization active.

The Sun has whirlpools, and some are only 20km wide

Solar physicists reported a population of tiny plasma vortices swirling across the Sun's surface, with the smallest measuring roughly 20 kilometers across.
