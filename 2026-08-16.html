Creator Economy🚩 Applies to You

YouTube doubles the price of admission to the Partner Program

YouTube announced on August 10 that new creators will need 8,000 qualified watch hours over 365 days — up from 4,000 — or 20 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days to start earning ad and Premium revenue. The company clarified the rules on August 14: only public long-form video, podcasts, and archived livestreams count. The bar rises February 1, 2027, and existing Partner Program members who accept the new terms by January 31, 2027 keep the old maintenance thresholds.

The aging brain is not the sealed fortress we thought

Stanford researchers reported this month that large numbers of immune cells from the bloodstream begin entering the human brain as early as middle age, undercutting the long-held view of the brain as immunologically isolated behind the blood-brain barrier. If that infiltration turns out to be causal rather than incidental, it reframes where interventions in neurodegeneration might plausibly start.

Solar physicists reported the discovery of extraordinarily small plasma vortices swirling across the Sun's surface — some only 20 kilometers across, at the very edge of what current instruments can resolve.
