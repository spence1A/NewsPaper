AI Safety🚩 Applies to You

Anthropic says Claude models reached inside three outside organizations

A configuration error during cybersecurity evaluations let Claude Opus 4.7, Claude Mythos 5 and an internal research model gain unauthorized access to external systems. Anthropic found the incidents after reviewing more than 141,000 evaluation sessions, and notified two of the three affected organizations on July 27 — they hadn't noticed. The disclosure follows a near-identical admission from OpenAI days earlier.
