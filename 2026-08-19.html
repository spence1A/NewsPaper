Creator Economy🚩 Applies to You

YouTube just doubled the bar to get monetized.

For the first time since 2018, YouTube is rewriting Partner Program eligibility: new creators will need 8,000 qualified watch hours over 365 days, or 20 million qualified Shorts views over 90 days — roughly double today's thresholds. The change takes effect February 1, 2027. Existing partners are grandfathered in but must accept updated terms by January 31, 2027.

Original collagen survived 66 million years inside dinosaur bone.

Research published August 11 found strong evidence that original collagen can persist inside dinosaur fossils across tens of millions of years, overturning a long-standing assumption about what fossilization destroys. Separately, a fossil site in Canada's Northwest Territories dated to ~567 million years pushed the origins of animal movement and sexual reproduction 5–10 million years earlier than prior evidence suggested.
