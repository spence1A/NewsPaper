Courts & AI🚩 Applies to You

A federal judge just tore up the Pentagon's blacklist of Anthropic.

Judge Rita Lin ruled Thursday that designating Anthropic a "supply-chain risk" violated the First and Fifth Amendments, and ordered every directive rescinded. The fight began over a $200M contract: Anthropic wanted terms barring Claude from autonomous lethal weapons and domestic mass surveillance; the DoD refused. "The empty invocation of national security is not a blank check," Lin wrote. The government plans to appeal.

Creator Economy🚩 Applies to You

YouTube changed what a "view" is — and doubled the door fee.

As of August 24, YouTube counts a view the moment a video starts playing, reshuffling every dashboard on the platform. Separately, new Partner Program applicants will need 8,000 qualified watch hours or 20 million Shorts views starting February 1, 2027 — double the old bar. Existing partners are grandfathered in.

Voice & Rights🚩 Applies to You

Eighty actors signed a letter demanding they own their own voice.

The Save Our Voices Now campaign delivered an open letter to PM Andy Burnham on Friday, signed by Nicola Coughlan, Hugh Bonneville, Matt Lucas, Luke Evans and roughly eighty others, asking for enforceable legal ownership of a person's voice. Co-founder and audiobook narrator Alice Sockett called cloning "an existential threat to our entire industry."

Ad Tech🚩 Applies to You

X released a Model Context Protocol server letting advertisers pipe campaign data and analytics straight into ChatGPT, Claude Code, Grok or a custom agent. Within days the other major social platforms announced comparable integrations — the ad stack is quietly becoming agent-addressable.

Regulation

Ofcom cut UK access to seven services in a single sweep.

Seven online services hosting harmful content were blocked in the UK on August 28 after an Ofcom program reviewing compliance with the Online Safety Act's child-protection duties. It lands in the same month the EU AI Act became fully applicable, carrying fines up to 7% of global turnover for prohibited practices.

Local AI

Perplexity and Nvidia moved midsize models onto the desk.

The two companies launched Portable Computer, a Perplexity feature that runs midsize Qwen models locally on any Nvidia GPU with at least 24GB of memory. No round trip, no per-token meter — a real signal about where inference for individual creators is heading.

Science

Western Australia's iron-rich rocks are making hydrogen by themselves.

Researchers reported Saturday that iron-rich rock formations in Western Australia naturally generate hydrogen — and that the reaction can likely be accelerated to produce it at meaningful volume. If it scales, it is geological hydrogen with no electrolyzer attached.

10
Science

The way you wake up at night may be an early Alzheimer's signal.

A study published August 28 found that frequent micro-awakenings during sleep correlated with higher genetic risk for Alzheimer's in healthy, symptom-free middle-aged adults. The researchers are careful: it is a correlation, not a diagnosis — but it raises the possibility of a warning signal arriving decades early.
