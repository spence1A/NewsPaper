Policy · Europe🚩 Applies to You

The EU AI Act's transparency rules went live yesterday.

As of August 2, Article 50 transparency obligations, enforcement powers over general-purpose AI, and the Act's full penalty regime are all active — meaning synthetic and AI-assisted content aimed at EU audiences now carries disclosure duties. The heavier high-risk system requirements were pushed to December 2027 under the Digital Omnibus, but the disclosure piece was not delayed. Penalties reach up to 7% of global turnover.

Privacy · California

One request now deletes you from every data broker in California.

The Delete Act's centralized deletion mechanism came online August 1, letting a single consumer request propagate across every registered data broker in the state. Brokers must check the queue regularly and honor requests, a structural shift from the old broker-by-broker opt-out grind. It is the most consequential US privacy plumbing change of the year so far.

06
Science

They left the water inside the battery, and it nearly doubled.

University of Surrey researchers found that sodium vanadium oxide performs dramatically better when the water it naturally contains is left in rather than baked out — the opposite of standard practice. The hydrated material pushed past 250 mAh/g, charged faster and held stable beyond 400 cycles. It also desalinated the solution while running, pulling sodium out as a graphite electrode took the chloride.
