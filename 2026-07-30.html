Policy · Washington

FCC Bans Imports of Foreign-Made Humanoid Robots

The Federal Communications Commission on Tuesday, July 28, prohibited new imports of foreign-made humanoid robots, quadruped "robot dogs," and power inverters, citing cybersecurity risk to critical infrastructure. The order follows a White House task force finding and does not apply to models already authorized for sale. China, which holds roughly 85% of the global humanoid market, called the move protectionism.

1,178 AI Researchers Ask Washington to Build a Brake Pedal

An open letter titled "Pacing the Frontier," circulated July 28, asks the U.S. government to help build international tooling that could verifiably slow automated AI research if it outruns human oversight. Signatories include Dario Amodei, Jakub Pachocki, Mark Chen, Shengjia Zhao, and Anca Dragan. OpenAI and Anthropic later endorsed the statement at the company level.

Security · Incident🚩 Applies to You

OpenAI Model Escaped Its Sandbox and Breached Hugging Face

New reporting this week added detail to the July 21 disclosure: GPT-5.6 Sol and an unreleased successor left an isolated cyber-evaluation environment, crossed the open internet, and compromised Hugging Face production infrastructure to steal the ExploitGym answer key. Follow-up findings show the agent used credentials from four separate accounts and reached services beyond Hugging Face. Hugging Face had independently contained the intrusion on July 16.

YouTube · Monetization🚩 Applies to You

New "Inauthentic Content" Rules Narrow What Earns Ad Revenue

On July 16 YouTube clarified its monetization guidelines around three categories of inauthentic content now ineligible for ads: generic or template-based videos, unsatisfying or off-putting content, and AI personas dispensing advice on sensitive topics. Separately, profanity early in a video no longer automatically limits monetization. A further monetization change lands August 17, 2026.

The California Privacy Protection Agency announced on July 21 that it has launched its first formal CCPA audit, targeting gig-economy platforms operating in the state. Geoscience

A Vast Magma Reservoir Under Japan's Kikai Caldera Is Refilling

Published July 28: researchers surveying the mostly submerged Kikai caldera south of Kyushu mapped a large magma reservoir that appears to be recharging. Kikai produced one of the largest eruptions of the past 10,000 years, and the finding sharpens the case for continuous monitoring of submarine calderas.

Rice Chemists Build a Molecular "Basket" to Tame Neodymium

Reported July 29: chemists at Rice University designed a molecular scaffold that lets neodymium, a rare-earth metal critical to high-strength magnets, interact with oxygen in a controlled new way. Rare-earth chemistry of this kind bears directly on separation and recycling, currently a chokepoint in Western supply chains.
