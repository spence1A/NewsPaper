Creator Economy · August 17
🚩 Applies to You

YouTube will count a view the instant playback starts.

Beginning August 24, YouTube registers a public view on the very first frame — no minimum watch time — across long-form video, livestreams and podcasts, matching the system Shorts already uses. Channel view counts will jump, in some cases dramatically, without any change in actual audience. Every historical benchmark, sponsor deck and CPM comparison you have is about to be measuring a different thing.

Artificial Intelligence · August 13
🚩 Applies to You

Anthropic is in talks to buy Decart for $6 billion.

Bloomberg reports the Claude maker is negotiating its largest acquisition ever — an Israeli startup whose software makes AI chips run more efficiently and cuts training and inference costs. Decart was valued near $4 billion in May, making this roughly a 50% premium. The deal is not final and could collapse, but the motive is plain: absorb surging Claude demand without buying proportionally more compute.

AI Safety · August 17

OpenAI dissolved its catastrophic-risk team.

The internal group charged with evaluating and mitigating catastrophic risks from frontier model development has been disbanded. It is the latest in a multi-year pattern of safety-focused teams at the company being restructured or absorbed elsewhere. The timing lands as OpenAI pushes frontier cyber capabilities into enterprise channels through Amazon Bedrock.

The AI video generation startup closed a round backed by Goldman Sachs, Intel, DST Global and Liberty Global. Science · August 14

Organic molecules found inside 66-million-year-old dinosaur bone.

Researchers reported recovering organic molecules preserved in dinosaur bone dating to the end-Cretaceous boundary — a result that pushes hard against long-standing assumptions about how quickly biological molecules degrade. If it survives replication, it reopens questions paleontology considered settled about what the fossil record can still tell us.

Creator Economy · August 10
🚩 Applies to You

YouTube is doubling the bar to join the Partner Program.

From February 1, 2027, new applicants need 8,000 watch hours in 365 days or 20 million Shorts views in 90 days — double today's thresholds. Existing partners keep their status and are unaffected. The practical effect is a much longer unpaid runway for anyone starting now, which reshapes the pitch to first-time creators.

A dedicated experience for ages 13 to 17 launched today, pairing tighter content restrictions with learning tools and optional parental controls. It arrives a week after Anthropic's Claude for Teachers, putting all three major labs into direct competition for the classroom in a single month.

Figma's Weave tools now run from inside Claude.

Starting this month, Figma Weave tools can be invoked from ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor through the Figma MCP server — the agent finds the tool, runs it, and returns the result in conversation. Figma also shipped a timeline with keyframes into Figma Design and 20+ AI image tasks. Thumbnail and channel-art workflows can now live in the same chat window as everything else.

Climate & Aviation · August 18

Operation Blue Skies launched over the North Atlantic.

Google, NATS and the UK Met Office began a 30-month, £5M program — billed as the first state-backed trial to avoid contrails across an entire oceanic airspace. Roughly 10,000 flights a year through Shanwick airspace will get altitude adjustments across two four-month winter trials, with Google contributing £1.4M in-kind for AI contrail prediction and satellite verification.

Policy & Privacy · August

The EU AI Act became fully enforceable — and Claude started watermarking.

The Act is now fully applicable, carrying fines up to 7% of global turnover for prohibited practices. In response Anthropic added watermarking to future Claude text outputs, stating no visible impact on quality, speed or pricing. Separately, California's DROP platform obliges data brokers to honor deletion requests as of August 1.
