OpenAI ships Astra, its most capable and most contested model

OpenAI released Astra on Thursday to customers in its Daybreak cybersecurity program, with paid-plan and API access following next week. President Greg Brockman called it the company's most intelligent and most aligned model yet. Google answers with Gemini 3.8 Flash — and a locked-down Cyber twin

Google shipped Gemini 3.8 Flash on September 2, its third Flash release in six weeks, plus a defenders-only 3.8 Flash Cyber variant. It posts 90.8% on Terminal-Bench 2.1, up from 81.6%, and beats Claude Opus 5 on three published benchmarks. Sept 3 · Science

Vesper bats carry two separate sets of antibody genes — a first in mammals

Researchers reported that more than 500 species of vesper bats possess two distinct sets of antibody genes, an immune architecture never previously documented in any mammal. Bats already tolerate viruses that devastate other species; a duplicated adaptive-immunity toolkit is a plausible piece of why.
