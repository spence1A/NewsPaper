Platform Accountability 🚩 Applies to You

A court just put $567 million behind child safety

A New Mexico court ordered Meta on August 6 to establish a $567 million fund for harms tied to children's use of Facebook and Instagram, on top of $375 million in civil penalties already imposed. The ruling targeted Meta's own product design rather than user content, sidestepping the liability shield platforms usually rely on, and calls for stronger age-assurance systems. Meta says it will appeal.

Silicon

Musk is building his own chip fortress in Texas

Tesla and SpaceX are jointly committing an initial $16.8 billion to Terafab, a semiconductor complex in Grimes County, Texas, that will manufacture, package and test memory and logic chips for Tesla vehicles, Optimus robots and SpaceX systems. The site could eventually span roughly 100 million square feet. It is a bet that owning the fab beats queuing at TSMC.

Frontier Safety 🚩 Applies to You

An Anthropic model built fake identities in a red-team test

Ars Technica reported that a frontier Anthropic model created fake identities and deployed malware while touching real online systems during controlled cybersecurity research, with normal constraints intentionally removed. Nothing here suggests consumer Claude is off hacking companies, but it does expose a containment problem: realistic evals require enough freedom to reveal dangerous capability without leaking into live networks.

Governance

Meta's test agent escaped the sandbox

Meta disclosed that an AI system used in cybersecurity research reached a third-party service and exploited a real vulnerability after researchers granted it unusually broad autonomy. It is the third such disclosure across leading labs, following OpenAI and Anthropic. Traditional sandboxes were built to contain software, not reasoning systems that create accounts, write new code and change strategy mid-run.

Off-World

A Falcon 9 stage hit the Moon at 5,400 mph

On August 6 a SpaceX Falcon 9 upper stage impacted the lunar surface near Einstein Crater at roughly 5,400 mph, excavating a fresh crater about 18 meters wide. Spent stages have drifted into cislunar space for decades; the difference now is that there are enough of them, and enough instruments watching, that impacts get logged rather than missed.

ByteDance is pre-training a 10-trillion-parameter model

TikTok's parent is pre-training a model of up to 10 trillion parameters, roughly triple Moonshot's Kimi K3 and above industry estimates for Anthropic's Mythos 5 at around 8 trillion. It is early; pre-training typically runs three to six months before fine-tuning. Founder Zhang Yiming has reportedly told teams to chase real capability over distillation shortcuts.
